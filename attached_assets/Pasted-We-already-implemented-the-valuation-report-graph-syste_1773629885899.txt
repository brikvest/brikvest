GOAL

We need to separate:
1. raw asset / land valuation value
2. investor pricing / performance value

Reason:
The price at which investors buy into a property is not always the same as the raw land value, because the investor-facing price may include additional costs such as:
- SPV costs
- legal costs
- valuation costs
- structuring/admin costs
- platform/deal packaging costs

Because of this:
- the land can appreciate
- while investor performance may still be flat or negative
- and the investor graph must reflect that correctly

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE

Do NOT use the same value for both:
- Land Appreciation graph
- Investment Performance graph

These two graphs must now be powered by different value bases.
