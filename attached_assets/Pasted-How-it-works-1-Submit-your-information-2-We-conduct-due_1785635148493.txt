Developer Onboarding Guide
Welcome to Brikvest — let's get you live.
4. You start selling
We're excited to get you onboarded to Brikvest.
To publish your first listing and start reaching thousands of middle-class buyers who want to invest in your land —
please provide the information below.
01 — COMPANY INFORMATION
Tell us about your business. This information is submitted once and only needs to be updated if it changes.
✓ Company name
✓ Company logo — your logo will appear on your store listing
✓ RC number — your Corporate Affairs Commission registration number
✓ Brief company description — a short paragraph about your business that buyers will see on your store page
02 — PROPERTY INFORMATION
Please provide details for your first listing. You can add more plots after your store goes live.
✓ Estate name
✓ Plot number
✓ Location — full address or nearest landmark
✓ Total land size — in square metres (sqm)
03 — FRACTIONAL SALES SETUP
This is how your land will be offered to buyers on the Brikvest platform.
✓ Partition size — how you would like the land divided. The minimum unit size is 5 sqm. For example: 50 units of
10 sqm, or 100 units of 5 sqm.
✓ Pricing per unit — your preferred price per unit. You set your own pricing.
Volume discount — optional but recommended
You can offer a lower price per sqm for buyers who purchase larger units. For example a buyer purchasing 50 sqm
could pay less per sqm than someone purchasing 10 sqm. This is entirely optional but often encourages larger
purchases and helps you sell out faster.
04 — MARKETING MATERIALS
These will be displayed to prospective buyers on your Brikvest store page.
✓ High-quality photos — clear photos of the land and the estate. The better the photos the better your listing
performs.
✓ Land description — a short paragraph describing the land, its location, and why it is a good investment.
✓ Estate description — a brief overview of the estate and any amenities or infrastructure in place or planned.
05 — PAYMENT DETAILS
Buyers pay you directly. Brikvest never holds your funds.
✓ Bank name
✓ Account name
✓ Account number
06 — DUE DILIGENCE
Before your property goes live on Brikvest our team will carry out a due diligence review.
✓ We will verify ownership and title documentation for the property.
✓ We will confirm the information submitted is accurate and consistent.
✓ Our team will contact you directly if any additional documents are required.
✓ Due diligence is non-negotiable — no listing goes live without passing this review.
This process protects both you and your buyers and gives investors the confidence they need to purchase through your
listing.
07 — WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
1 We receive your submission
Our team reviews everything you've sent and confirms receipt.
2 Due diligence is completed
We verify the property and company information. We will contact you if anything additional is needed.
You sign the partnership agreement
3
Before going live you will sign the Brikvest Developer Partnership Agreement which outlines both parties'
responsibilities.
4 Your store goes live
We set up your branded store on the Brikvest platform with your listing published and ready for buyers.
You start selling
5
Share your listing with your existing buyers and agents. We will also provide a branded flyer you can distribute. Then
you can start selling.
