# BRIKVEST – P2P MARKETPLACE ENHANCEMENT

## Objective

Update the existing Brikvest P2P Marketplace to enable users to purchase land holdings from other existing Brikvest users, regardless of whether those holdings have been explicitly listed for sale.

**IMPORTANT: Do not create a new marketplace or navigation tab.** Expand the existing P2P Marketplace to accommodate two distinct purchasing options:

1. **Listed Land:** Land that an existing owner has voluntarily listed for sale.
2. **Unlisted Land:** Land currently owned by Brikvest users that has not been listed for sale but for which interested buyers can submit unsolicited purchase offers.

Both categories should operate within the existing P2P Marketplace while providing a clear distinction between listed holdings and holdings available to receive unsolicited offers.

Preserve the existing application's architecture, visual design, database integrity, authentication, portfolio management, and other functionalities.

---

## 1. P2P Marketplace Interface

Retain the existing P2P Marketplace tab in the application's main navigation.

Within this marketplace, introduce two clearly differentiated sections:

**Listed Land**

Display land holdings that owners have actively listed for sale.

Retain the existing listed-land purchasing functionality and integrate the updated transaction, payment, and verification requirements where applicable.

**Unlisted Land**

Display eligible land holdings already owned by Brikvest users, even where those users have not advertised or listed their holdings for sale.

Users must not be required to list their holdings before another user can submit a purchase offer.

Include a short explanation:

"Explore existing land holdings and submit purchase offers directly to owners, even when their land is not listed for sale."

Both sections should have a consistent interface and match Brikvest's existing branding.

## 2. Unlisted Land Discovery

When a buyer selects the Unlisted Land section, display the available land assets held by existing Brikvest users.

Each asset should display:

- Land name.
- Location.
- Property details.
- Total land area.
- Ownership unit or fractional holding information.
- Number of existing owners, where appropriate.

When the buyer selects a particular land asset, display the existing ownership positions associated with that property.

For each owner, show:

- Anonymous owner reference (Owner 001, Owner 002, etc.).
- Total quantity of land owned.
- Quantity currently available to receive offers.
- A "Make an Offer" button.

**All existing owner identities must remain anonymous.**

Do not publicly display usernames, legal names, email addresses, phone numbers, KYC details, or private account information.

Owners must automatically be eligible to receive purchase offers on qualifying holdings without needing to create a listing.

Do not show holdings that have already been transferred, are otherwise ineligible for transfer, or are fully reserved under another accepted transaction.

## 3. Offer Submission

When a buyer clicks "Make an Offer", open an offer submission interface.

Allow the buyer to:

- Select the anonymous ownership position.
- Specify the land quantity they wish to purchase.
- Enter their proposed purchase price.
- Review the proposed transaction.
- View the applicable Brikvest transaction fee.
- Confirm and submit their offer.

The buyer must not be able to submit an offer for a quantity exceeding the owner's available holding.

Generate a unique offer reference and store the offer in the database.

Once submitted:

- Notify the selected landowner through email.
- Generate an in-app notification.
- Display the offer in the buyer's transaction history.
- Set the status to "Awaiting Seller Response".

The 30-minute payment countdown should NOT begin when an initial offer is submitted. It should only begin after both parties have agreed to the transaction.

## 4. Brikvest Transaction Fee – 2%

Apply an additional 2% Brikvest transaction fee to every successfully negotiated purchase.

The fee must be calculated using the final agreed land purchase price.

Use the following calculation:

**Land Purchase Price = P**

**Brikvest Transaction Fee = P × 0.02**

**Total Buyer Payment = P × 1.02**

For example:

| Description | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Agreed land purchase price | ₦1,000,000 |
| Brikvest transaction fee (2%) | ₦20,000 |
| Total amount payable by buyer | ₦1,020,000 |

The 2% transaction fee must be added to the agreed purchase price, not deducted from the seller's agreed amount.

Display the fee breakdown clearly during offer submission, negotiations, payment, and in transaction records.

Whenever an offer or counteroffer changes the purchase price, automatically recalculate the 2% fee and total buyer payment.

Use accurate monetary calculations and ensure the correct amount is stored in the database.

## 5. Seller Email Notification

Whenever an existing owner receives a purchase offer, automatically send an email notification.

Suggested email:

**Subject: New Purchase Offer Received – Brikvest**

Dear [First Name],

You have received a new offer from a Brikvest user interested in purchasing part or all of your existing land holding.

Land: [Land Name]

Quantity Requested: [Quantity]

Proposed Purchase Price: [Amount]

Offer Reference: [Reference Number]

Please click below to review the offer.

**[REVIEW OFFER]**

You can accept, submit a counteroffer, or decline the offer.

The review button should securely redirect the owner to the relevant offer within the Brikvest application.

The recipient must be authenticated before responding.

Neither party should be able to access the other's personal details.

## 6. Seller Response and Negotiation

After opening an offer, the owner should have three options.

**A. Accept Offer**

The seller accepts the proposed purchase price and land quantity.

The system should:

- Confirm that the seller still owns the proposed quantity.
- Reserve the agreed quantity.
- Notify the buyer.
- Generate a pending purchase transaction.
- Start the 30-minute payment countdown.

**B. Counteroffer**

The seller may propose a different purchase price or quantity.

The buyer should receive an email and in-app notification containing the revised terms.

The buyer should be able to:

- Accept the counteroffer.
- Submit another counteroffer.
- Decline the offer.

Allow negotiations to continue until both parties reach an agreement or either party declines.

Recalculate the 2% Brikvest fee whenever the negotiated purchase price changes.

**C. Decline Offer**

The seller declines the proposed transaction.

Update the offer status to "Declined" and notify the buyer.

No payment or ownership transfer should occur.

Maintain a complete negotiation history for administrative and audit purposes.

## 7. Land Reservation and Double-Selling Prevention

Once an offer is accepted, the agreed land quantity must immediately be reserved.

For example, if an owner holds 500 sqm and accepts an offer to sell 300 sqm:

| Holding | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Total ownership | 500 sqm |
| Reserved for accepted offer | 300 sqm |
| Remaining available holding | 200 sqm |

Other buyers may submit offers for the remaining 200 sqm but must not be able to complete a transaction involving the reserved 300 sqm.

The system must prevent the same quantity from being reserved or sold through multiple simultaneous transactions.

If the accepted transaction expires, is cancelled, or is rejected without proceeding to completion, the reserved quantity should be released.

Use database-level transaction controls to ensure accurate ownership and reservation balances.

## 8. Mandatory 30-Minute Payment Window

Once the buyer and seller agree to a purchase, activate a 30-minute payment window.

The countdown must begin at the exact time the final offer is accepted.

Direct the buyer to their portfolio, where the pending transaction should be displayed.

Show:

- Land name.
- Quantity being purchased.
- Agreed purchase price.
- Brikvest transaction fee (2%).
- Total amount payable.
- Official Brikvest bank account details.
- Unique payment reference.
- Payment deadline.
- Live 30-minute countdown.
- Upload Payment Receipt button.

The countdown must be calculated and enforced on the server.

**The buyer must pay and submit proof of payment within 30 minutes.**

If proof of payment is successfully uploaded before the deadline, update the transaction status to "Payment Under Review".

Keep the land reserved while the administrator verifies payment.

Uploading a receipt does not automatically constitute payment approval.

## 9. Automatic Expiry and New Offer Requirement

If the buyer fails to submit proof of payment before the 30-minute deadline:

- Automatically expire the transaction.
- Cancel the existing payment instructions.
- Release the reserved land.
- Return the quantity to the owner's available holding.
- Update the transaction status to "Expired – Payment Deadline Missed".
- Notify the buyer and seller.

**The buyer must submit an entirely new offer if they still wish to purchase the land.**

Do not allow the buyer to reactivate the expired transaction or automatically extend the payment window.

The seller must independently accept the new offer.

Upon acceptance, generate a new transaction reference and a new 30-minute payment window.

If money is received after expiry, flag it for administrative review and reconciliation rather than automatically transferring land ownership.

Ensure expiry works even when users have closed their browsers or logged out.

## 10. Payment Instructions and Receipt Upload

All payments must be made directly into Brikvest's designated bank account.

Do not facilitate direct buyer-to-seller payment.

The buyer must transfer the full amount, including the additional 2% Brikvest fee.

Once the buyer uploads their payment receipt:

- Save the receipt securely.
- Record its submission timestamp.
- Mark the transaction as "Payment Under Review".
- Notify the administrator.
- Retain the reservation pending verification.

Administrators must verify that the full transaction amount has actually been received.

A receipt upload alone must never trigger ownership transfer.

## 11. Admin Payment Verification

Expand the existing admin dashboard to accommodate P2P Marketplace transactions.

Administrators should be able to:

- Review listed and unlisted land transactions.
- Access buyer and seller identities.
- Review the offer and negotiation history.
- View the agreed purchase price.
- View the additional 2% fee.
- Confirm the total amount payable.
- View uploaded payment receipts.
- Verify that funds have been received.
- Approve or reject payment submissions.
- Review the buyer's KYC status.
- Authorise ownership transfers.
- Investigate expired transactions and disputed payments.

Payment verification must be based on actual funds received, not solely on uploaded receipts.

## 12. Buyer KYC Verification

Before completing an ownership transfer, verify the buyer's KYC status.

If the buyer has already completed KYC, proceed once the payment and administrative requirements have been satisfied.

If the buyer has not completed KYC, direct them to Brikvest's existing verification process.

Display:

**KYC Verification Required – Ownership Transfer Pending**

This applies to newly registered users and existing users with incomplete KYC.

KYC processing should not reset or extend the 30-minute payment deadline. The buyer must still submit proof of payment within the required window.

Once payment evidence is submitted on time, the transaction may remain pending while KYC is completed.

Do not transfer ownership until KYC has been approved.

## 13. Ownership Transfer

Once the following requirements are satisfied:

- Both parties have agreed to the purchase.
- Proof of payment was submitted before the deadline.
- The full payment, including the 2% fee, has been independently verified.
- The buyer has successfully completed KYC.
- The agreed land quantity remains reserved.
- The administrator has approved the transfer.

Automatically:

1. Deduct the sold land quantity from Seller A's portfolio.
2. Credit the exact quantity to Buyer B's portfolio.
3. Update the underlying ownership records.
4. Record the transaction value and Brikvest's 2% fee separately.
5. Record the total payment received.
6. Update both users' transaction histories.
7. Mark the transaction as "Completed".
8. Notify both parties.

If the seller transfers their entire holding, remove that holding from their active portfolio but preserve its historical record.

If the seller transfers only part of their holding, retain the remaining balance.

The transfer must be atomic and idempotent to prevent duplicate ownership credits or deductions.

## 14. Transaction Tracking

Both buyers and sellers should have access to transaction tracking within their portfolios.

Include the following statuses:

- Offer Submitted
- Awaiting Seller Response
- Counteroffer Received
- Offer Accepted
- Payment Pending
- Payment Under Review
- Payment Verified
- KYC Verification Required
- Transfer Pending
- Completed
- Declined
- Expired – Payment Deadline Missed
- Payment Rejected
- Cancelled

Each transaction must display the relevant land, quantity, final agreed price, transaction fee, total payable, date, and reference number.

Preserve a permanent audit trail of offers, counteroffers, payment verification, administrative actions, and ownership changes.

## 15. Privacy and Security

Maintain anonymity between buyers and sellers, particularly within the Unlisted Land section.

Never disclose another user's:

- Full name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- KYC documentation.
- Bank information.
- Private account identifiers.

Only authorised Brikvest administrators should access sensitive transaction information.

All offer-related actions must require authentication and appropriate authorisation.

Protect offer links, payment receipts, ownership data, and transaction history from unauthorised access.

Ensure users cannot manipulate offer quantities, fee calculations, payment statuses, or ownership balances through frontend requests.

## 16. User Experience and Navigation

Maintain the existing P2P Marketplace tab.

Within the marketplace, display two clear purchasing categories:

**Listed Land | Unlisted Land**

For Unlisted Land, the intended transaction journey is:

P2P Marketplace → Unlisted Land → Select Land → View Anonymous Owners → Select Owner → Make Offer → Seller Accepts / Counters / Declines → Offer Agreed → Land Reserved → 30-Minute Payment Window → Buyer Pays Purchase Price + 2% Fee → Upload Payment Receipt → Admin Payment Verification → Buyer KYC Check → Admin Approval → Ownership Transfer Completed.

If the payment deadline is missed:

Transaction Expires → Land Reservation Released → Buyer Must Submit a New Offer.

The interface should remain intuitive, responsive, and consistent with Brikvest's existing design.

## 17. Technical Implementation and Testing

Before implementation, inspect the existing Brikvest codebase, database relationships, portfolio functionality, authentication, notification service, payment infrastructure, KYC workflow, and administrative dashboard.

Reuse existing components wherever possible.

Do not rebuild the application or remove existing functionality.

Implement and test:

- Separation of Listed Land and Unlisted Land within the existing marketplace.
- Unsolicited offers to existing landowners without requiring listings.
- Anonymous ownership displays.
- Offer submission and counteroffer negotiations.
- Automatic calculation of the additional 2% transaction fee.
- Fee recalculation following counteroffers.
- Automatic land reservation.
- Prevention of double-selling.
- Server-enforced 30-minute payment deadlines.
- Automatic transaction expiry.
- Mandatory new offers following expiry.
- Receipt submission and secure storage.
- Admin payment verification.
- Buyer KYC enforcement.
- Accurate portfolio ownership transfers.
- Transaction history and audit logs.
- Late-payment reconciliation.
- Email and in-app notifications.

Do not implement placeholder buttons, fabricated payment confirmations, or simulated ownership transfers.

The completed update should provide a fully operational secondary land marketplace within Brikvest's existing P2P Marketplace, allowing users to acquire both actively listed and unlisted land holdings through secure, transparent, and administratively verified transactions.
