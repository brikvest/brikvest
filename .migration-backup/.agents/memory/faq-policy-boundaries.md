Treat the supplied Brikvest FAQ document as the content authority for public FAQs, not developer-pricing FAQs. Flag material differences from other site copy rather than silently rewriting claims or changing business logic.

**Why:** The supplied FAQs expressly promise a most-recent-valuation buyback after the maturity sale window and a valuation-based refund for ownership disputes; the About page disclaims guaranteed liquidity. Publishing the FAQ copy did not authorize reconciling those policies or altering legal agreements, fees, KYC, valuation, or resale rules.

**How to apply:** Require explicit business approval before harmonizing conflicting investment-policy statements. Keep a content change separate from changes to legal agreements or transaction behaviour.
