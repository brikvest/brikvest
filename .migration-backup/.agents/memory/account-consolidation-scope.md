---
name: Duplicate investor consolidation boundary
description: Identity and retained-evidence limits for administrative consolidation
---
Duplicate-account consolidation is not authorization to transfer investments
between different people or correct legal/financial history.

**Why:** The user requires explicitly verified duplicate identities, preservation
of approved destination KYC, historical payments, unit allocations, valuation
basis and certificate evidence, and no real-account changes during implementation.

**How to apply:** Keep identity verification independent of shared first names.
Treat active resale work, retained legal history, signed evidence and unsupported
dependencies as blockers, not reasons to disable database guards. Operational
contact changes must retain prior contacts in attributable audit evidence. Test
only with disposable data; schema preparation does not authorize a live merge.
